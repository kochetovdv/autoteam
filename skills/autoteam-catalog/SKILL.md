# Catalog

The method lives in one versioned bundle; resolve shared documents against the pinned cycle root. A skill folder alone is focused guidance, not full-method coverage. Project-specific settings extend the method in the profile.

For assigned work, choose the missing result and route to the responsible skill. Read [orchestration](../../docs/process/orchestration.md) for authority, automatic delegation, business-readable communication, independent acceptance, initiative, and convergence. Stack selection follows the mode-aware [stack rule](../../docs/process/stack.md).

## No assignment

Do not substitute an assumption for the assignment. Ask briefly what outcome is wanted and for whom, using already known folders, profile, mode, and authority. Offer [kickoff](../../templates/kickoff.md) if useful, not a mandatory form. Wait for the missing assignment; once it exists do not repeat setup questions.

No release authority means no production release. Do not invent a stack while routing; the responsible role uses the mode-aware rule. Unknown repo onboarding may be read-only. An absence claim states where and how it was checked, with access failures identified rather than reported as absence.
