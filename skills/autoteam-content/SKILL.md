# Content

Content is a delivery channel for positioning, not "copy for copy's sake". Voice and messaging — from `autoteam-positioning`; facts — from the product; distribution — per the `autoteam-growth` channel map.

**Input.** Positioning, ICP, channel map, product facts, the goal of the content unit (reach, trust, conversion, retention). **Output.** Content units fitted to the platform's format, a content plan with goals, reuse of one piece of source material across several formats. **Forbidden.** Invented facts, numbers, testimonials, and case studies; clickbait that breaks trust; keywords every other word; publishing without the human if the platform speaks for the human/company. **Evidence.** Every fact — from the product or a source with a link; every unit has a goal and a metric. **Stop.** None. No source material — take it from the product or mark the gap with a question, don't invent. **Next.** Page → `autoteam-ui`. SEO/GEO requirements and distribution → `autoteam-growth`. Results → `autoteam-analytics`.

## Order

1. Unit goal: why it exists in the funnel (learned → believed → tried → returned). No goal — don't write.
2. Topic from the intersection: ICP pain × product expertise × what people search for (queries, community questions).
3. Format for the platform: article, post, thread, email, video script, talk. Don't post an article as-is to social media.
4. Voice: from positioning — vocabulary, tone, what we never say. One voice across all platforms.
5. Structure: conclusion first, self-contained paragraphs (fit for citation in AI answers), question subheadings where appropriate.
6. Reuse: one piece of source material (study, case study, release) → several formats, not the other way around.
7. Calendar: rhythm beats spikes; a unit without a date and a platform is not scheduled.

Honesty: present product experience as product experience, not as an "independent opinion". Generated illustrations and voiceover — per platform rules.

Content with zero source material (no product/company facts in the input): build copy on process-level ASSUMP facts, honest statuses, and disclaimers; every placeholder goes into a single "replace by the human" list; invented case studies, numbers, and testimonials are forbidden as always. Fact hunger is not a license to invent.

Artifact: `docs/product/content-plan.md` in the **product**.
